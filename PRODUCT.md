# Ahmed Home Renovation — product context

- **Platform:** web
- **Stack:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`), hosted on GitHub Pages. Only external resource: Cal Sans from Google Fonts.
- **Surface mode:** Persuade (a one-page site for a local trade business).

## Users
- Homeowners in Windsor, LaSalle, Tecumseh, Amherstburg and surrounding areas (Ontario, Canada) who need painting, drywall work or small renovations. Source: the business's Facebook post, 2026-10-20.
- They mostly arrive from Facebook on a phone, inside Facebook's in-app browser (inferred from how the business markets itself: Facebook groups only).
- Second audience: the owner, who receives the site for free and should recognise their own business in it within one phone screen.

## Purpose
Give a business with no web presence a real site that turns a visitor into a free-estimate request.
